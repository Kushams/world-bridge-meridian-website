Pace Gallery London  ·  Planning your trip

Kylie Manning

The Mudlarkers

13 October – 14 November 2026

Welcome, and thank you for joining The Mudlarkers.

Pace Gallery has asked World Bridge Meridian, its official travel partner, to arrange your journey to London: flights, ground transport, accommodation and every detail around them, for you and up to two companions. Everything is arranged in one place on our website, in a few short steps. This email walks you through them.

Please do not book anything yourself

Travel booked elsewhere is not reimbursed without Pace Gallery’s prior written approval. Leave the bookings to us, and we will take care of them for you.

Have these ready (about 10 minutes)

  • Full names of everyone travelling, exactly as on their passports (you and up to two companions)
  • Passport number and expiry date, and a photo of the passport page
  • Departure city and your preferred airport
  • Room preference for companions: shared or separate
  • Dietary needs and allergies, and an emergency contact
  • Extended stay (optional): if you would like to stay for the whole performance week, say so in the form. Any extension is subject to the Gallery’s confirmation.

Your steps, from sign-up to your itinerary

1

Open the exhibition page

Tap the button below. It opens the page for Kylie Manning: The Mudlarkers on our website, with everything about the show in one place.

Open the Mudlarkers page

2

Create your free account

Tap Sign in at the top of the page, then choose how you would like to join:

  • Continue with Google: the quickest way, one tap, or
  • Email and password: choose a password, then tap the confirmation link we email you, or
  • Email me a sign-in link: no password needed.

Please use the same email address Pace Gallery has for you, so we can match you to the exhibition. Keep to the same phone or computer for steps 1 to 3.

3

Tap “Plan This Trip”

On the Mudlarkers page, tap Plan This Trip. Your travel form opens with the exhibition already filled in.

Plan my trip

4

Fill in your travel form

It has seven short parts. You can answer what you know now and add the rest later by replying to us.

  1. Your traveller details and passport (upload the passport photo page if you can)
  2. Exhibition and travel preferences
  3. Health and dietary needs
  4. Emergency contact
  5. Your companions, if any (up to two)
  6. Your itinerary overview (optional, you can leave this blank)
  7. Special requests and anything else we should know

When you are done, tap Send. You will receive a confirmation email straight away.

Your passport details are kept private and are visible only to our travel team.

5

We build your itinerary

Your travel coordinator reviews your form and sends your proposed itinerary to your account. The Gallery confirms your four performance dates in writing, and we build your travel around them.

Check My World Bridge on our website for messages, itineraries, tickets and documents to sign. You will also get an email each time there is something new.

6

Travel to London

Once you arrive and the Gallery confirms your presence, your fee and approved costs are paid to you within 24 hours, as set out in your agreement with Pace Gallery.

Stuck at any step? Reply to this email, write to info@worldbridgemeridian.group, or message Client Services on WhatsApp at +1 302 627 3325, and we will help right away.
