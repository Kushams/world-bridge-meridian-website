# 29. INTENT RULE

Conversely, never manufacture intent.

If the source says:

> "Planning a honeymoon in Greece"

> Intent Type: Honeymoon

> Intent Type: None Identified

Do not turn assumptions into facts.

# 30. LEAD STATUS

The discovery engine should distinguish:

### DISCOVERED

Potential lead found.

### ENRICHED

Additional relevant information found.

### DUPLICATE

Existing lead already exists.

### OUT OF SCOPE

Does not fit WBM's target structure.

### READY FOR OUTREACH

Enough useful information exists for the WBM outreach workflow.

Important:

"Ready for Outreach" does NOT mean the person has agreed to travel.

It only means the lead contains sufficient information for the separate outreach team to evaluate/contact according to its own process.
